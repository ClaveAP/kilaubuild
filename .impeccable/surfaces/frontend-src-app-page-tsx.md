# Public homepage redesign

- Scope: `frontend/src/app/page.tsx` and its public navigation, sections, forms, and footer.
- Mode: Persuade with an Experience-led portfolio.
- Audience: property owners in Depok and Jabodetabek who need proof before trusting a contractor.
- Job: understand the payment mechanism, inspect real work, and book a free survey or start a WhatsApp conversation.
- Proof: authentic project imagery, live project content, progress percentages, specific client reviews, and the existing company contact details.
- Constraints: preserve the API contracts, real assets, KilauBuild blue palette, Indonesian language, accessibility, mobile performance, and all current form behavior.

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** Treat the site as a living field register where every payment follows visible work. Refuse the familiar contractor landing page made from a generic skyline hero followed by rows of interchangeable icon cards.

**OWN-WORLD:** Cobalt ink, milk white, deep navy, pale site-paper blue, and full-color documentary photography. Typography is compact, direct, and architectural. Rules, tabs, stamps, progress rails, and caption blocks behave like site records; containers are used only when they organize evidence.

**STORY:** The visitor sees how KilauBuild reduces payment risk, checks completed and active work, understands the service scope, hears specific client experiences, and books a free survey. The primary action is scheduling a survey; WhatsApp remains the fast alternative.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** A documentary project photograph owns most of the frame. A concise promise sits in a solid navy field, with the three-stage payment rail “Bangun → Periksa → Bayar” visible before scrolling. The survey action is high contrast and the next project proof peeks into view.

**FORM:** Register Termin Lapangan, grounded candidate 7 from seed `9d2d98d0`. Scale hierarchy borrows the confidence of a screened poster, the photo sequence borrows montage discipline, the layout snaps to whole units like tiled narrative panels, and large quiet fields preserve active negative space.

**FINISH:** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

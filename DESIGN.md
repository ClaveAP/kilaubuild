# KilauBuild — Design Language & Tokens

## 1. Visual World
- **Atmosphere**: Professional contractor meets premium modern architecture. Trustworthy, clean, structured, and modern.
- **Brand Colors**:
  - `brand-primary`: `#005592` (Dominant brand blue for hero gradients, buttons, footers, primary links)
  - `brand-hover`: `#0066AE` (Lighter blue for active states and hover highlights)
  - `brand-accent`: `#66B2E0` (Sky blue for high-contrast CTA badges and buttons)
  - `brand-dark`: `#004475` (Deep navy for dark contrast containers)
  - `brand-soft`: `#EBF4FA` (Soft sky tint for card backgrounds and stats counters)
  - `brand-tint`: `#E3F2FD` (Subtle highlight background)
  - `surface-white`: `#FFFFFF`
  - `text-dark`: `#111827`
  - `text-muted`: `#4B5563`
  - `border-light`: `#E5E7EB`

## 2. Typography
- **Heading**: `Roboto` / `Plus Jakarta Sans`, font-semibold to font-extrabold.
- **Body**: `Inter`, sans-serif, font-normal to font-medium.
- **Numbers / Metrics**: Monospace or bold sans-serif with high visual emphasis.

## 3. Interaction & Animation
- Smooth transition (`transition-all duration-300 ease-in-out`).
- Card elevation on hover (`hover:shadow-xl hover:-translate-y-1`).
- Active navbar state with underline or color transition upon scroll.
- Responsive mobile drawer menu with smooth backdrop blur.
